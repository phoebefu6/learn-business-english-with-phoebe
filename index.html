Learn Business English with Phoebe

If English is your second or third language, you probably learned the formal kind: "kindly revert", "please find attached herewith", "I just wanted to inform you". It feels safe. To a busy reader in another office it reads as distant, unsure, or simply hard to act on. Six sessions on plain, confident workplace English: emails that get answered, speaking up in meetings, numbers said out loud, and a phrase bank you keep. One constructed running case, and your own email measured against 400 real business emails in your browser. The goal is English any reader understands on the first pass, not English that sounds native.

The running case is Mei Lin Goh, a constructed data analyst at Harbourline, a constructed logistics company in Singapore. Her analysis is excellent and her emails get slow replies. She is invented for teaching, and every page says so. Alongside her, every build-along runs on your own emails and your own meetings.
